About DayMaker

DayMaker provides local weather and radar alongside optional fictional weather personas and a local Meme Studio. Weather and official safety information take priority over entertainment.

Weather data is provided through the app's OpenWeather backend. Radar availability depends on region and network access. Read observation times and coverage messages; missing data does not mean there is no hazard.

The fictional Karen, Frat Bro, Grandpa, Politician, and 2-Year-Old personas are entertainment. They do not represent real sponsors or public officials. Sample content is labeled in the app.

DayMaker is in development. These pages do not imply store availability, ad approval, or a paid subscription.
