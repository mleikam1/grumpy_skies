Privacy information

This page describes this development implementation. Advertising is disabled by default. Production activation requires a completed publisher privacy review and updated store disclosures.

Weather and location

When you choose a location, the app uses it to request weather from its existing backend. Device location is requested only through your permission flow; manual location search is available. Selected locations, preferences, and recent weather may be saved on your device. Backend hosting and weather providers process requests under their own terms.

Your memes

Personal photos, captions, and editable meme documents stay in the local Meme Studio store. Choosing Share or exporting a backup transfers the content to the destination you select. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; save backups before clearing storage or uninstalling.

Advertising and choices

The mobile test integration uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. It requests ads only when that platform permits requests and the app's additional safety and configuration gates pass. Privacy choices appear in Settings when required. Non-personalized advertising does not bypass consent. Apple tracking permission is separate and this implementation does not prompt for it.

Ad requests do not include your coordinates, photos, captions, or private documents. The Google SDK may process device, network, diagnostic, and advertising data under Google's advertising privacy information. Content filtering is not a guarantee about every creative.

Local frequency limits and hashed completion identities prevent repeat interstitials. An optional comparison group can keep eligible users ad-free when measurement is permitted. No client analytics event uploader is configured in this build. Web advertising remains unavailable until an approved provider and consent implementation are connected.

Questions and data controls

You can change the selected weather location and app preferences in Settings. Meme documents are managed in My Memes. Keep project backups before removing app data. Production privacy contact details and retention disclosures still require publisher verification; do not treat this development page as a completed store privacy submission.
